Privacy

Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Crystal Hive has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It doesn't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier, and nothing about how you play leaves your phone.

Motion

The game reads your phone's motion sensors only to steer the ball. That data stays on the device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Replay clips

If you choose to save a replay clip, the game writes it to your Photos library. It never reads your photos.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data, such as Game Center leaderboards or optional analytics you can turn off, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details first.

Support

Email support@clarkestudios.com

Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened. A real person reads every message, usually within two business days.

The ball doesn't respond to tilt

Allow motion access when the game asks. If you said no, open the game's settings and switch to touch drag, or allow motion again in the iPhone Settings app.

The ball drifts when I hold my phone still

Tap the screen with two fingers to set "level" to however you're holding your phone.

Restoring a purchase

On the main menu, open Full Hive or Cores and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases belong to your Apple ID, so they carry over to a new phone.
