Privacy

Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

When analytics is enabled, Crystal Hive sends anonymous gameplay events (for example, levels started and finished) and your device model to GameAnalytics. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. You can turn analytics off at any time in the in-game Settings.

What we do not collect

There are no accounts. We do not collect your name, email, contact list, location, photos or advertising identifier. Motion sensor data is used on your device only, to steer the ball.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center. Your scores and display name are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Photos

If you choose to save a replay clip, it is written to your Photos library. The app does not read your photos.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions: support@clarkestudios.com

Support

Need help? Email support@clarkestudios.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

The ball does not respond to tilt. Allow motion access when asked. If you declined, close the app, then re-open it and tap to start; or check Settings > Crystal Hive.

Restore purchases. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Turn off analytics. Settings in the game has an analytics toggle.
