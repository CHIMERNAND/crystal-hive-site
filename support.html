Crystal Hive Support

Need help? Email support@clarkestudios.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

The ball does not respond to tilt. Allow motion access when asked. If you declined, close the app, then re-open it and tap to start; or check Settings > Crystal Hive.

Restore purchases. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Turn off analytics. Settings in the game has an analytics toggle.
